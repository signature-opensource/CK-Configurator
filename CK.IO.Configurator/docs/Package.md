# CK.IO.Configurator

The commands of the configurator: the catalog of what can be configured in a published profile of a World
(`IOpenCatalogCommand`, `IGetConfigurableTypesQCommand`, `IGetClosureQCommand`, `IGetCommandsQCommand`), targeted by
their source (`ICommandCatalogSource`), and the queries that list the branches and the profiles of a World
(`IGetBranchesQCommand`, `IGetProfilesQCommand`).

See the [README](https://github.com/signature-opensource/CK-Configurator/blob/stable/CK.IO.Configurator/README.md).
