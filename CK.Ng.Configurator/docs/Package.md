# CK.Ng.Configurator

The Angular side of the configurator: `NgConfiguratorPackage`, its catalog page (`CatalogPageComponent`, at
"ckomposable/catalog": the configurable types of a published profile of a World, what a selection brings in, the
commands, the packages) and its editor page (`EditorPageComponent`, at "ckomposable/editor": the central `ckonf.json` of
a solution and its deployments, typed and checked by its catalog, edited with their comments kept).

See the [README](https://github.com/signature-opensource/CK-Configurator/blob/stable/CK.Ng.Configurator/README.md).
